# Elisa Card Website — Project Brief

## Purpose

Create a warm, personal and calming bilingual website for Elisa Card.

The website should first help visitors understand Elisa's story, values and philosophy. From there, visitors should be able to naturally discover the different ways they can work with her.

## Core Message

Elisa Card raised five boys alone and experienced major unexpected challenges throughout her life.

Her message is:

Life is meant to be taken lightly.

Unexpected challenges are part of life, but we can learn methods and techniques that help us avoid overwhelm, regain inner calm and move forward with greater confidence.

Elisa teaches practical wellbeing methods to help people navigate these moments while protecting their inner peace.

## Visitor Journey

The website should follow this general journey:

1. Discover Elisa and her story.
2. Understand her philosophy and approach.
3. Recognize how her work can help.
4. Explore the different ways to work with Elisa.
5. Take a simple next step.

## Website Pages

The website must be available in English and French.

Main pages:

- Home
- About
- Coaching
- Retreats
- Speaking & Webinars
- Blog
- Contact

## Ways to Work With Elisa

Visitors can discover:

- Coaching
- Wellbeing programs
- Retreats
- Speaking engagements
- Webinars

## Tone of Voice

The writing should feel:

- Warm
- Human
- Hopeful
- Calm
- Personal
- Inspiring
- Practical
- Easy to understand

Avoid overly complicated language.

## Visual Direction

The website should be:

- Mostly white
- Spacious
- Calm
- Elegant
- Personal

Use small touches of orange and pink.

Do not redesign the visual identity without Elisa's approval.

## Bilingual Rule

Every visitor-facing text must exist in both English and French.

When English copy is added or changed, the corresponding French copy must also be added or changed.

## Content Workflow

Some website texts will initially be marked TODO.

Work through these texts one section at a time with Elisa.

Ask Elisa one question at a time before writing or changing important website copy.

## Development Rules

- Astro
- Tailwind CSS
- Hosted on Vercel
- Do not change the technology stack.
- Do not change the established folder structure.
- Do not make website changes directly on the main branch.
- Use a separate branch and pull request for each website change.
- Keep each change small and easy to review.
